How to become a Bengaluru landowner

  1. Pick your cells. Zoom into any ward and tap. Each cell is about 165 m across and costs ₹10.
  2. Write your rant. Choose a frustration and add up to 60 characters for the city to read.
  3. Pay by UPI. No stamp duty, no khata charges, no "processing fee".
  4. Get your allotment letter. Your plot goes live on the map within 24 hours.

Frequently asked questions

Is this BDA approved?

No. It is approved by everyone who has ever paid a 10-month deposit.

When do I get possession?

Immediately. Unlike that pre-launch flat you booked in 2019.

Is it near the metro?

Every plot is near an upcoming metro. We just don't say which decade.

Can I build a house on it?

You can build a rant on it. That is more than your current rent allows.

Is this real land?

No. Ten Rupee Estates is satire. Your plot is a spot on a map and a line of text. The frustration is real.

What can't I write?

Politics, hate against any community, abuse, real names of people or landlords, phone numbers, betting or adult content. We refund anything we reject.
