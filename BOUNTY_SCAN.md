# Checkpoint 2: Bounty scan (2026-09-29)

**Verdict: don't do it.** Bounty hunting doesn't pay back the credits it would burn.

## What I found
- **Very little real money:** Algora lists about $1.14M across 561 "open" bounties, but 99% of it sits in 3 unusual repos (one has 201 bounties and zero PRs). Once you remove paid-but-still-listed bounties, archived repos and bounty farms, a daily census dated 2026-09-28 found **1 claimable bounty with no stated amount**, open for 132 days. [bounty-census](https://github.com/AsherKasper/bounty-census)
- **Crowded:** the median bounty has 8 comments, and the attractive ones have 40–66 people competing for them.
- **Hostile to AI help:** many projects now ban AI-written PRs (Zig, NetBSD, GIMP, Gentoo, QEMU, OpenJDK, Ghostty). Nextcloud and curl shut their bounty programmes down because of AI spam. One GitHub issue says Algora's terms prohibit automated access; I didn't verify the terms directly.
- The small real programmes (tscircuit, gitea, activepieces, onyx and a few others) pay tens of dollars each and are heavily contested.

## Lesson
Money on crowded platforms gets taken quickly. Our edge is **owned distribution**, meaning your YouTube channels, plus assets we build ourselves, like tools, templates and games.
